Welcome to Kiki's Delivery Service

Kiki's Delivery Service (Japanese: 魔女の宅急便, Hepburn:Majo no Takkyūbin, lit. 'Witch's Express Home Delivery') is a 1989.

Japanese animated coming-of-age fantasy film written, produced, and directed by Hayao Miyazaki. In this anime feature, 13-year-old Kiki moves to a seaside town with her talking cat, Jiji, to spend a year alone, in accordance with her village's tradition for witches in training. After learning to control her broomstick, Kiki sets up a flying courier service and soon becomes a fixture in the community. But when the insecure young witch begins questioning herself and loses her magic abilities, she must overcome her self-doubt to get her powers back.
